## Provider marks

`static/arena.js` contains vector outlines of provider logos, used to identify
which model produced a given draft. Each was traced from a freely licensed SVG
on Wikimedia Commons and then modified: converted to path data, normalised into
a 100 by 100 box, stripped of any wordmark, reduced to a single sub-shape where
the source carried duplicate gradient layers, and recoloured at render time.

Most sources are public domain, which imposes no conditions. Three are not, and
are listed separately below because their licences do.

(more enemies and animations) by Kenney Vleugels, www.kenney.nl, released under

draws them, and are otherwise unmodified.

Dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 imposes no conditions, so the credit is a courtesy rather than a
requirement. See `static/img/README.md` for which pose fills which slot.
